3. THE RTL BUGS — and there were more than you saw
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Both had one cause: I wrote literal symbols into a right-to-left text flow and
the browser reordered them.

  ARROWS pointed →, which in RTL points back at the step already read. Now ←.

  THE MINUS landed AFTER the digits — "157,205.96−" — because a bare sign
  beside a formatted number gets moved by the bidi algorithm. The sign and the
  number are now one isolated LTR run, so it reads "−157,205.96".

I checked for the same pattern everywhere rather than only where you pointed,
and found four more:

    ReportAlerts     the overspend "+" had the identical problem
    SavedSimulations the price-change arrow
    Reports          the raise-log arrow
    Transactions     the transfer arrow

One I deliberately did NOT change: the export's period label. That goes into a
filename and a provenance sheet, not into Hebrew text flow, so → is right
there.
