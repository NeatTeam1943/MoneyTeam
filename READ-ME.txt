SAVED SIMULATIONS — I went with your second option
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
You offered two: keep the ids and report the missing ones, or freeze
everything and add "apply to current state". The second is right, and the
reason is that the first fixes only half the problem.

Storing ids catches items that were DELETED. It says nothing about items that
CHANGED — a price filled in, a quantity edited. Those reopen silently at a
different total than the one on screen when it was saved, which is the exact
failure the id approach was meant to avoid.

So the whole picked set is frozen: name, price, quantity, category, program,
plus the account balances. A saved simulation always reopens showing what it
showed.

"APPLY TO CURRENT STATE" is a separate button, and its value is in being
honest about what did not survive. Four outcomes, deliberately not merged:

    unchanged   carries over silently
    changed     carries over at TODAY's figures, and says so — the point of
                re-applying is to see current numbers, so keeping the old ones
                would defeat it
    bought      has a transaction against it. Not a plan any more; counting it
                again would double it
    missing     deleted or cancelled

"Already bought" is kept apart from "no longer on the list" because they mean
opposite things to the reader. Merging them into "could not apply" would make a
completed purchase look like a loss.

The report also gives the total difference: "saved at ₪2,560 — today it is ₪180
more". And when nothing changed it says so, which is the answer people are
hoping for.

8 assertions in scripts/audit-money.sh cover all of it, including that bought
is not counted as missing and that the delta counts only rows that carry.

THE OTHER FIVE
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. GUESTS SEE BUDGETS                                    migration 44
   budgets_guest and ledger_lines_guest, following the pattern migration 16
   set — separate views rather than relaxed policies. A guest sees the plan,
   the spend, what is left. Requests, reservations and the lock are SKIPPED,
   not attempted: an RLS denial inside Promise.all aborts the whole load, and
   the page would show nothing instead of showing less.

2. ARCHIVED GOALS STOP COUNTING                          migration 44
   Worse than it looked — Dashboard and Reports never filtered them at all, so
   an archived goal kept subtracting its reservation in three places. Fixed at
   the source with an `active_goals` view: a convention ("remember to filter")
   is exactly what failed here.

3. REQUESTED TOTAL ON THE DASHBOARD
   A count answers "is there a queue"; the total answers "can we afford it".

4. EXCEL EXPORTS
   A new budgets export plus every existing sheet reformatted — 14 sheets, none
   raw. Widths measured from content, real number formats so values stay
   NUMERIC (a currency string sorts alphabetically — 9 after 10), autofilter
   everywhere, and a summary sheet that opens with the overspends.

   Checked rather than assumed: this build of SheetJS drops cell styling on
   write, and writes no frozen pane whatever key you set. I unzipped the output
   to confirm both, then removed the freeze code rather than leave a line that
   looks like it works.

6. SHARING AS PDF
   window.print(), not html2canvas + jsPDF — saves ~250kB and produces a real
   PDF with selectable text. The print stylesheet hides controls, forces
   background colours (without it Chrome drops them and the overspend
   highlighting prints white on white), and adds a team/season/date header.
