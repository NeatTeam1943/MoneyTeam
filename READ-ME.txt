The simulation PDF, rebuilt. No SQL.

WHAT WAS WRONG
I printed the working screen with its controls hidden. That was the wrong idea,
not a styling miss: the screen exists to BUILD a plan — checkboxes, filter
chips, a selection table — so printing it produced a page of tick boxes with
truncated columns, no totals, no balances, and nothing about what the plan
actually does.

NOW
A separate document. The working view is hidden outright when printing; the
document is the only thing on the page.

Ordered by what a reader asks first:

  1. the cost           planned spend, expected income, projected total —
                        as a band across the top, so the figure is the first
                        thing the eye lands on rather than a row in a table
  2. what it breaks     accounts going negative, budgets going over. Before the
                        itemisation, because if a plan sends an account
                        negative that is the thing to say first
  3. account balances   current, change, projected
  4. goals after it     target, what survives, what is still short
  5. the items          last. No checkboxes, no status chips, no links — a
                        printed page cannot be clicked, and every one of those
                        was noise in the first attempt

Plus a header with the team, season and date, and a footer saying it is a
projection and nothing was recorded — a shared PDF of figures that look like
transactions is worth labelling.
